About Yipi Bear

Creating therapeutic stories to help children navigate their emotions through the adventures of Momo Yuzuki.

Our Mission

At Yipi Bear, we believe that every child deserves to feel understood and supported in their emotional journey. Our mission is to create personalized therapeutic stories that help children aged 5-8 understand and manage their emotions through the adventures of Momo Yuzuki, our lovable red panda protagonist.

Each story is carefully crafted to address specific emotional challenges while maintaining a fun and engaging narrative that children can relate to and learn from.

Our Story

Yipi Bear was born from a simple observation: children often struggle to express and understand their emotions, and traditional methods of emotional education can sometimes feel too clinical or disconnected from their world.

Our founder, Sarah Chen, combined her background in child psychology with her passion for storytelling to create Momo Yuzuki - a character that children could relate to and learn from. What started as a small project has grown into a platform that helps thousands of children worldwide navigate their emotional landscape.

Today, Yipi Bear continues to evolve, incorporating feedback from parents, educators, and most importantly, the children who read our stories. We're committed to making emotional education accessible, engaging, and effective for every child.
